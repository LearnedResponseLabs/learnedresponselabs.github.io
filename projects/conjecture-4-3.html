Galashin’s Conjecture 4.3

Galashin’s measurement map turns angles on a circle into a point of a positroid cell’s critical variety; his Conjecture 4.3 says it is a homeomorphism. For every loopless cell, the map is the gradient of an explicit convex potential, so it is one, and its inverse is a convex minimization.
