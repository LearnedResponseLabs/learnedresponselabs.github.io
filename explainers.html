Galashin’s Conjecture 4.3

The idea, in seven stepsHow the argument works, at the level of intuition: angles make a point, the point is the slope of a convex bowl, winding cycles keep the bowl convex, and minimizing reads the angles back. Each step has a picture and leads to the details.→The playgroundEvery example of the reading edition, live: choose one from the parts of the paper, change its cell, angles or directions, and watch its objects recomputed in the browser. Each agrees with Lean’s own output on every setting checked.→A cell, in picturesType any cell, as a window of up to nine labels, and follow it through the boundary circle and its strands, the crossing graph, the pair classes, the necklace, the matrices from the exponents to the incidence matrix, and the winding-one cycles; on the running example, move the angles and watch admissibility, the cotangent terms and the inverse. Every object agrees with Lean’s own values on all 5,913 cells with up to seven labels.→Figures from the formalization123 pictures drawn by Lean from its own values, by part of the paper: the boundary and its strands, the crossing graph, necklaces, the matrices, the winding-one cycles, and the potential and its inverse on the running example. Each links to the items of the reading edition that use it.→The laboratoryGeometry behind the critical measurement map: recover the angles from the point, a convex landscape, cycle contributions, ideal volume, the non-top shift, collisions and necklace coordinates. Numerical and exact-integer laboratories with recorded examples, each linked to the statement of the paper it illustrates. The collision laboratory is a separate three-label example, not the finite atlas of a companion manuscript in preparation.→

Python calculations download Pyodide from its CDN; the laboratories include recorded examples if it cannot load.
