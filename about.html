About

Who runs the program, how the work is produced and checked, and what the status words mean.

The program

Learned Response Labs is an independent research program founded by Adam Guetz. It explores AI-assisted mathematical discovery, formal verification and human-legible proof auditing, as a collaboration between its founder and AI systems. Its current research is in positive geometry, chiefly the positive Grassmannian and related spaces.

How the work is made

The proofs, text, software, atlases, and other artifacts of this program were primarily generated by AI coding harnesses (OpenAI Codex and Anthropic Claude Code) operating adversarially: results produced by one system were frozen, independently rederived, and hostilely audited by the other before internal acceptance, with machine-checked Lean 4/Mathlib components where noted. Adam Guetz designed and directed the research program: the choice of problems, the harness and adversarial-audit architecture, and the cross-domain references that shaped it.

Each released paper has a reading edition, which sets every formal statement beside what checks it (a frozen Lean statement or a pinned exact certificate) and every step without one beside its source. The author checks the paper’s main statements, and the definitions they use, against their Lean text in the edition. A paper’s latest version is posted here while that check is in progress, labelled “Internal certification pending”. A version is archived on Zenodo and announced only once the check is complete, when the label reads “Internally certified”. External review is pending, and reviews received will be noted with the paper’s public reading edition.

What the status words mean

Proof candidateA complete written proof that has not had external mathematical review. Every result on this site is one unless it says otherwise.
Checked in Lean 4The statement is frozen apart from its proof, and an independent tool (Comparator) confirms that the proof proves exactly that statement, using only the standard axioms.
Internal certification pendingThe author is checking the paper’s main statements, and the definitions they use, against their Lean text in the reading edition. Corrections appear as new versions, listed on the paper’s page.
Internally certifiedThe author checked them, for the version named on the paper’s page. Only certified versions are archived on Zenodo.

Each paper’s claim manifest also uses the program’s evidence tiers: PROVED_DRAFT (a written proof at the internal tier), PROVED_AUDITED (one that passed a recorded internal audit), VERIFIED (an exact finite computation, pinned), NUMERICAL (floating-point evidence), CONDITIONAL (proved under a stated open hypothesis), IMPORTED (a cited result), OPEN (a stated gap) and NOT_CLAIMED (deliberately not asserted). None of them means externally established.
