About

Who runs the program, how the work is produced and checked, and what the status words mean.

The program

Learned Response Labs is an independent research program by Adam Guetz on the positive Grassmannian and its critical varieties. Its first released project is Galashin’s Conjecture 4.3; later projects join the site as their papers are released, each with a reading edition.

How the work is made

Learned Response Labs is a research collaboration between the author and AI systems. The proofs, text, software, atlases, and other artifacts of this program were primarily generated by AI coding harnesses (OpenAI Codex and Anthropic Claude Code) operating adversarially: results produced by one system were frozen, independently rederived, and hostilely audited by the other before internal acceptance, with machine-checked Lean 4/Mathlib components where noted. The author designed and directed the research program: the choice of problems, the harness and adversarial-audit architecture, and the cross-domain references that shaped it.

Before release, the author reviews the claim statements of each paper in its reading edition, which sets every formal statement beside what checks it (a frozen Lean statement or a pinned exact certificate) and every step without one beside its source; a release goes out only when the author has confirmed that every item says what the paper says. External review is pending, and reviews received will be noted with the paper’s public reading edition.

What the status words mean

Proof candidateA complete written proof that has not had external mathematical review. Every result on this site is one unless it says otherwise.
Checked in Lean 4The statement is frozen apart from its proof, and an independent tool (Comparator) confirms that the proof proves exactly that statement, using only the standard axioms.
Reviewed internallyBefore release, the author confirmed item by item, in the paper’s reading edition, that each statement says what the paper says.

Each paper’s claim manifest also uses the program’s evidence tiers: PROVED_DRAFT (a written proof at the internal tier), PROVED_AUDITED (one that passed a recorded internal audit), VERIFIED (an exact finite computation, pinned), NUMERICAL (floating-point evidence), CONDITIONAL (proved under a stated open hypothesis), IMPORTED (a cited result), OPEN (a stated gap) and NOT_CLAIMED (deliberately not asserted). None of them means externally established.
