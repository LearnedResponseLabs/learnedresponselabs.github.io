A Convex Bowl 5: Winding cycles

4:28 · MP4, 16 MB

An obtuse angle δ makes its Hessian weight cot δ negative, so Ψ is not convex edge by edge. A cycle of crossing edges that goes once around (exactly one wrapped edge) has angles summing to π. On such a cycle the cotangent cycle inequality makes the cycle's form ≥ 0, with equality only for constant shifts.
