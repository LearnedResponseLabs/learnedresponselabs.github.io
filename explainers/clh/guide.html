A reading path through the geometry

These are new visual experiments built around the Conjecture 4.3 paper (CLH) and the numerical interfaces of ogcanon. They are not a new proof, an independent mathematical audit, or a promotion of the papers’ claim status.

1. See the actual change of coordinates

The mesh and inverse lab uses the complete gauge chamber for f=(3,4,5): θ₁=0, 0<θ₂<θ₃<π. It computes C=Meas(θ), performs the left twist, reads log-necklace differences, and compares them with B log sin δ. Only then does the inverse solver reconstruct θ from the measured R(C).

The circle triangle, the source mesh, and the target mesh share one selected point. The ellipse is a tangent, first-order deformation glyph. Source mesh coordinates θ/π are dimensionless; target coordinates are the selected components of the gradient defined with respect to radian angles. We do not treat these as an isometry.

2. Inspect the mechanism, not just eigenvalues

The cycle assembly lab uses exact crossing edges and finite, exact edge covers by winding-one cycles. Eigenvalues and energies are numerical. A whole-cycle selection can introduce additional zero modes, but should not create negative eigenvalues. The edge-removal countercontrol deliberately leaves the hypotheses.

The dense-five margin control samples one triangular face of the four-dimensional gap simplex. Its exact formula for μ₅⋆ is the one the companion manuscript (in preparation) states. Evaluating it next to an eigenvalue is corroboration, not a regeneration of the exact Bernstein/Matrix–Tree certificate.

3. Make the underemphasized volume interpretation visible

The ideal-volume lab follows the paper’s treatment of hyperbolic volume (Equation (5), p. 5, and the paragraphs after it). The coned polygon is displayed using boundary vertices of a Klein ball. The equatorial circle and north-pole apex are a visualization of the polygon and the ideal point ∞. Exploding pieces is a display transform only. Hyperbolic volume comes from the Lobachevsky angle formula, never from the Euclidean volume of the displayed tetrahedra.

The antiderivative normalization is L(u)=−Λ(u)−u log 2. On one winding-one cycle Σδ=π, hence Ψ=−Volume−π log 2. All potential/volume displays use this declared normalization.

4. Use a real non-top source-to-target computation

The critical shift lab uses τ=(1,5)(2,6)(3,8)(4,7), electrical f=(6,8,7,9,10,12,11,13), and its shift. It holds θ₁=0, θ₄=0.3π and varies θ₂=aπ, θ₃=bπ; matching partners are separated by π/2. The two-dimensional sample mesh is a section of a three-dimensional τ-isotropic critical family. The underlying unconstrained crossing quotient has dimension seven. The matching’s full network cell dimension is five; none of these spaces is substituted for another.

The source and target clouds use separately fixed PCA projections of L1-normalized Plücker coordinates. Distances and overlaps in those displays are not intrinsic. The selected point is computed through actual inverse recovery; the background mesh is direct forward evaluation on shared angles. The τ-isotropy-breaking control illustrates why an ordinary measurement cannot generally stand in for the shifted dual.

5. Inspect a local boundary model honestly

The collision microscope computes θ=(0,επu,επ). Its projective measurement tends to [1−u:u:1], despite all ordinary angles tending to zero. This is a concrete top-cell family with an inner shape ratio—not the full cyclohedral compactification and not the finite certificates of the companion manuscript (in preparation).

The per-stratum certificates of the companion manuscript (in preparation) are not part of this suite. That manuscript reports the finite scope and counts; they are retained as reported metadata in data/scope.json, and no node-level atlas was synthesized from them.

6. Trace the exact integer identity

The necklace lab evaluates the crossing-pair exponent −1{p∈Iᵣ,q∉Iᵣ}, the cyclic forward difference, and the row relabeling by the inverse residue permutation. Noncrossing columns are zero on necklace rows, following CLH Proposition 10. The corrupt-exponent control creates an exact discrepancy. This is finite integer verification for the chosen fixtures, not the uniform proof.

Runtime

Production custom computations run in a module-type Pyodide worker. The worker requires sys.platform == 'emscripten' before declaring itself ready. NumPy is loaded with loadPackage. Recorded examples are explicitly labelled when Python is unavailable. No browser flag turns on a remote computation service.

Official runtime references: module workers and package loading. Runtime version is pinned in runtime-config.json.

Changes from upstream

The ogcanon source is unchanged. The adapter rejects invalid affine angle differences rather than folding arbitrary input modulo π. The numerical inverse adds explicit damping, complete accepted-iterate records and failure reporting. A solver failure is a solver failure, not a theorem counterexample. The ogcanon float64 method is not an exact proof kernel.
