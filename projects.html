Galashin’s Conjecture 4.3

For every loopless positroid cell, Galashin’s critical measurement map is the gradient of an explicit convex potential, so it is a homeomorphism and its inverse is a convex minimization. A proof candidate, with its combinatorial and convexity steps checked in Lean 4.
