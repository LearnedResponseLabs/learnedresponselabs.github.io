The regular cyclic point maximizes Plücker participation on the positive Grassmannian

Plücker participation measures how evenly a point of the Grassmannian spreads its weight across its Plücker coordinates. On the positive top cell, the paper gives a proof candidate that the maximum is attained at the regular cyclic point and only there, and that it equals an explicit product of squared cotangents. Its reading edition sets each formal statement beside what checks it.

Learned Response Labs is an independent research program founded by Adam Guetz. It explores AI-assisted mathematical discovery, formal verification and human-legible proof auditing; its current research is in positive geometry, chiefly the positive Grassmannian.
